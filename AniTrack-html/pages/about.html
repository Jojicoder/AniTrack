What is AniTrack?

AniTrack is a personal tracker for anime and manga fans. Save your collection, rate series, write reviews, and discover new titles — all in one place.

Features

  • Track anime & manga with reading/watching status
  • Rate series on a 1–10 scale
  • Write personal reviews and notes
  • Browse and search the full works catalog
  • Get handpicked recommendations
